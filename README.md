# Executive E-Commerce Sales & Profitability Dashboard

## Project Overview
An interactive business intelligence and commercial performance dashboard built using **Streamlit** and **Plotly**. The application allows business stakeholders to monitor top-level KPIs (Total Revenue, Profit, Profit Margin %, and Transaction Volume), analyze monthly revenue trajectories, and evaluate sub-category profitability across customer segments.

---

## Key Features & Analytics
* **Executive KPI Cards:** Real-time metrics tracking Revenue, Net Profit, Profit Margin (%), and Total Orders.
* **Interactive Slicers & Filters:** Dynamic sidebar filters allowing drill-downs across geographical regions and product categories.
* **Monthly Revenue Trajectory:** Interactive time-series line chart tracking monthly revenue momentum.
* **Segment & Category Profitability:** Multi-dimensional bar and donut charts identifying high-volume vs. margin-dilutive product lines.
* **Underlying Data Viewer:** Embedded interactive data table with sorting and filtering capabilities.

---

## Tech Stack
* **Framework:** Streamlit
* **Data Manipulation:** Python, Pandas, NumPy
* **Interactive Visualizations:** Plotly Express
